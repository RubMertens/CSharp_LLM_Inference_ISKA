RMSNorm — in Code

The diagram you just dragged, as 12 lines of C#.

  1. Sum the squares — total magnitude of the vector.
  2. Mean, then square root: the root mean square. Epsilon keeps a zero vector safe.
  3. Divide every element by it, then scale by the learned weight.

Note what's missing: no mean subtraction, no variance. That's why it's cheaper than LayerNorm.
